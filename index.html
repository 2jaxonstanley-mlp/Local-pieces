Solo Chess

Perfect Run

Solve 30 Solo Chess puzzles as fast as you can. No hints, no undo, no restarts. The timer runs for the whole run.

  1. Capture a piece with every move until only one piece remains.
  2. Each piece can capture a maximum of 2 times per puzzle.
  3. If a king is on the board, it must be the final remaining piece.
  4. Every piece looks the same, and any piece can capture any piece. Pawns capture diagonally, either way.
  5. If you get stuck with two or more pieces left, that puzzle is lost. Wins and losses both move you on to the next puzzle.
